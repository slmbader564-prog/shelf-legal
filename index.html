Shelf Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Shelf ("the app") is a calm, offline logic puzzle game. This policy explains what the app does, and does not do, with your information. In short: Shelf keeps your progress on your device. It has no accounts, shows no ads, contains no analytics or crash-reporting code, and runs no servers of its own, so the developer never receives your personal data.

Data we collect ourselves

None. Shelf stores the following only in the app's private storage on your device, and the developer never receives any of it:

  • Your settings (theme, sound, haptics, play mode, language choice, and the daily reminder and its hour if you turn it on).
  • Your puzzle progress: which rooms you have settled, stars, best times, mistakes, and any room in progress.
  • Your daily puzzle history and streak.
  • A small record of what you own from the Google Play purchase (a store product id and a timestamp), kept so the app works offline.
  • The date the app last asked you to rate it, so it does not ask too often.

Advertising and analytics

Shelf shows no ads. The app contains no advertising code and does not access your device's advertising identifier. It has no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK and no third-party tracking of any kind. We do not sell or share your data, because we do not collect it.

Other network access

Shelf plays fully offline. The only features that talk to anything beyond your device are the following, each either transactional or started by you:

  • Google Play Billing processes the app's one optional, one-time purchase (Shelf Complete) and restores it if you reinstall or change devices. The transaction is handled entirely by Google. Shelf never receives your payment card details, only confirmation of what you own. Google's handling of payment information is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play In-App Review may show the standard Play rating card at a few chosen moments, at most about once every 60 days. Shelf passes it no information about you or your play, and is not told whether you rated the app or what you said.
  • Sharing is always started by you. When you tap Share on your daily result, Shelf creates a picture of that day's solved shelf plus a short line of text (the date, your result and your streak) and hands them to Android's share sheet. Where it goes is your choice, and from that point the app you pick handles it under its own policy. Shelf keeps no copy beyond a temporary file in its cache.
  • Send feedback opens your own email app with a message addressed to the developer. It contains the app version and build number, and nothing is sent unless you write and send the email yourself.
  • Privacy policy in Settings opens this page in your browser.

Shelf does not use Google Play Games sign-in, cloud save or leaderboards, and has no account system.

Permissions

Shelf's installation package declares only the permissions its features need: billing (Google Play purchases), internet and network-state access (used by Google Play Billing to reach Google), and vibration (the tactile feedback, which you can turn off in Settings). Only if you turn on the optional daily reminder (in Settings, Daily shelf, or when the app offers it after a three-day streak) does Shelf ask Android for permission to show notifications; the reminders are scheduled on your device, are put back after a restart, and nothing about them leaves your device. It does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or advertising identifier.

Your data, in your control

Because everything Shelf stores lives on your device, you control it. Uninstalling the app removes all of its local data. Shelf lets Android include your progress and settings in your phone's own encrypted backup and in phone-to-phone transfer, so they can come with you to a new phone. That backup is run by Android and your Google account, follows your device's backup settings, and the developer never receives it. Purchase records are kept by Google Play under your Google account, not by us.

Delete your data

Shelf has no account and keeps no copy of your data on any server, so you can delete it yourself at any time:

  1. Open your device Settings.
  2. Go to Apps, then Shelf.
  3. Tap Storage, then Clear storage (or Clear data) to erase your progress and settings but keep the app, or tap Uninstall to remove the app and all of its data at once.

Children's privacy

Shelf is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The app shows no ads and has no way for players to communicate with one another. If you have a concern about a child's use of the app, contact us using the details below.
